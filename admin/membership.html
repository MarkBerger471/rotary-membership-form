The board, and how each of them is asked about an application. Tick Email, WhatsApp or LINE per person - they get the same two links whichever way it arrives, and a vote lands in the application log the same way.

Names, email addresses, numbers and LINE names are read from Member Contacts and are only edited there, so there is one copy of each. Who is on this list is decided there too, by ticking someone as a board member.

Email leaves the moment an application arrives. WhatsApp and LINE go out from your own accounts, so they wait for the senders running on your Mac.
